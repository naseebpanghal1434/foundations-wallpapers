A BST orders keys: whole left subtree < node < whole right. Search, insert: walk one child, O(height). Inorder walk emits sorted keys (plate 57). Unbalanced, height is n and you have a slow sorted list.

What

Average O(log n) if random or balanced. Worst O(n) on sorted inserts. Successor of x = leftmost of right, or the last ancestor you turned left from. Inorder is sorted keys.

Why

Search, floor/ceil, kth smallest (inorder count), validate, LCA in a BST (walk until split). Ordered map internals (plate 125).

Trap

Inserting sorted data into an unbalanced BST — it becomes a list. Checking only immediate children for “is BST.” Duplicate policy: usually left or disallow; say it.
