FrontendTarget · three phases

Capture down. Bubble up.

A click walks the tree twice: capture window → target, then bubble target → window. Most listeners sit on bubble; capture:true is the other pass — order is the whole trick.

up
What

Three phases, one event object. target is the origin; currentTarget is the listener’s node.

Why

Modals, menus, “click outside.” You pick the phase so a parent can run before or after the child.

Trap

Binding capture and wondering why the parent ran first. onclick= only bubbles. stopPropagation in capture so the child never hears it — surprise.
