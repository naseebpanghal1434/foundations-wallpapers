Choose. Recurse. Undo.

Backtracking is DFS on a decision tree. Template: if done, record a copy of path; for each choice: append, recurse, pop. The pop is the whole trick — mutate one array, don’t copy at every step. Prune when a partial path cannot succeed. Time is the size of the tree (often exponential).

What

DFS on choices. Undo restores state so the next sibling sees a clean path. You copy only when recording an answer. Time is the tree, often 2n or n! — say it.

Why

The parent of plates 182–186. If they say “all configurations,” this, not DP. DP is count/opt. Backtrack is the list. Name the choice, the undo, the prune in that order.
