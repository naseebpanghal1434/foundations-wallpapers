SystemGeohash · quadtree · drivers

Nearby is a geo hash, not a scan.

“Cars within 2 km” is not WHERE abs(lat-?) < 0.02 on 1e6 rows. Geohash precision 6 ≈ 1.2 km × 0.6 km. Nearby = that cell + 8 neighbors, then a true-distance filter. Live: SET driver:9 gh EX 15. The trip row in the DB is truth.

1e6 rows · unindexed float

Scan

SELECT * FROM drivers WHERE abs(lat-?) < 0.02 AND abs(lon-?) < 0.02 then Haversine in app every ping stored forever on the hot path

A bounding box without an index is a full scan with extra trig. Fine for a demo. Fatal at city scale.

vs

Prefix + 8 neighbors

Geohash

gh = geohash(lat,lon,6) ~1 km cell GET members of gh + 8 neighbors filter true km SET driver:9 gh EX 15 GEOADD also works

A car 10 m over the cell edge lives in a neighbor. Heartbeat TTL (plate 222). Then match on car type / ETA.

What

Spatial index + TTL presence. Heartbeat updates the cell; a trip locks the driver. Recalc ETA on that small set. Quadtree / S2 / H3 — say neighbors.

Why

Uber / Yelp nearby. They want the index, the 8 neighbor cells, and why lat/lon BETWEEN is not enough at 1e6 rows.

Trap

Bounding-box SQL on an unindexed float. One geohash with no neighbors (miss the car 10 m over the edge). Storing every ping forever on the hot path.
