bcrypt cost 12 is 2^12 iterations.

Password hashes must be slow (plate 31): bcrypt cost N ≈ 2^N Blowfish setups. Cost 12 is ~200–400 ms on a 2020s CPU; 13 is ~2×; argon2id is the current memory-hard winner. Store algorithm+cost+salt+hash in one string ($2b$12$…); verify compares the digest, not == on the password.

Work factor should grow with hardware. Timing-safe compare (plate 359). ~3 bcrypt/s/core at cost 12 vs 1e9 MD5/s.

What

Adaptive KDF. Cost is a parameter you store and can raise on next login. argon2id if you can choose today.

Why

Offline dump resistance. 1e9 MD5/s vs ~3 bcrypt/s/core. Stolen rows should not be passwords.

Trap

Cost 4 in prod. Truncating bcrypt at 72 bytes silently. HMAC-SHA256 as a password hash without a KDF.
