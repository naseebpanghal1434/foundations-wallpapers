DatabaseRC · RR · 40001

Isolation is what others see.

Isolation is which concurrent writes you are allowed to see. Atomicity (plate 07) is all-or-none of your writes. Anomalies: dirty read, non-repeatable read, phantom. Postgres default: READ COMMITTED. MySQL InnoDB default: REPEATABLE READ. PG SERIALIZABLE is SSI; conflict → SQLSTATE 40001, retry.

dirty read

RU

You read a write they have not committed. They ROLLBACK. You acted on a ghost. Postgres does not really do this.

non-repeatable

RC

Read Ada 100, they COMMIT 70, you read 70 in the same txn. Postgres default. REPEATABLE READ forbids it.

phantom

RR

COUNT(*) was 4; they INSERT; you count 5. Next-key locks (InnoDB RR) or SSI stop this.

retry

40001

SERIALIZABLE / SSI: snapshot plus conflict detection. Abort, retry the txn. More isolation → more waits/aborts.

What

RU < RC < RR < serializable. MVCC (plate 96) gives a snapshot instead of many read locks. Lost update: read-modify-write under RC without SELECT … FOR UPDATE or a version column.

Why

“I checked stock then sold twice” is isolation, not missing BEGIN. Interviews: name the anomaly, pick the level, mention retry.

Trap

SERIALIZABLE on every query “to be safe” (throughput dies, 40001 surprises). RC plus a read-modify-write with no lock/version — lost update, cousin of plate 43.
