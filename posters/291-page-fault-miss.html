OS#PF · major · minor

A page fault is a miss in the map.

Access a VA with no valid PTE → #PF. Minor fault: the page is already in RAM (CoW, lazy alloc) — just install a PTE. Major: disk/SSD (demand from file or swap). SIGSEGV if the map does not allow it (NULL, NX on a jump).

What

The fault handler is the lazy mapper. rss grows on touch, not on mmap.

Why

Why startup “allocates” 1 GiB instantly (virtual) then faults in. Why swap destroys tail latency.

Trap

mmap 1 TB and thinking RAM is gone. Touching every page at once (memset warmup) as a stampede of majors.
