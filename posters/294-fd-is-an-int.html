What

The user-space handle is an int. The kernel object is a file. Two fds can share one offset (dup) or not (two opens).

Why

Redirection, daemonizing, “too many open files.” Leaking fds exhausts the table, not the disk.

Trap

close(1) then open() — logs go to the file you just opened. Forgetting CLOEXEC and leaking sockets across exec. Assuming fds are unique across processes.
