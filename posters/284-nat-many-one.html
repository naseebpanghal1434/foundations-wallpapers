NAT is many insides, one outside.

NAT: many private hosts share a public IP. The box rewrites source IP:port and remembers a mapping — inbound needs a hole punch, a port forward, or a relay.

What

A stateful rewrite. Outbound easy, inbound hard. The public IP is shared; the port is the identity.

Why

“It works on my laptop” behind a home router. WebSockets and long-poll timeouts are often mapping expiry.

Trap

Assuming the client’s IP is unique (logs, rate limits — plate 201). Server-initiated TCP to a phone. Trusting X-Forwarded-For without a hop policy.
