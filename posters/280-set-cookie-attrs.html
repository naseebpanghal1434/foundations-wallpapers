NetworkSet-Cookie · host-only

Secure, HttpOnly, Path. That's the cookie.

The cookie is a response header: Set-Cookie: name=value plus attributes. Secure means HTTPS only; HttpOnly means no JS (plate 232); Path and Domain scope where it is sent — missing flags are the vulnerability.

1

Set-Cookie: sid=…; Secure; HttpOnly; Path=/; SameSite=LaxMax-Age=3600. SameSite is also an attribute (plate 233). __Host- needs Secure and Path=/.

What

Server-set credentials with a send policy. The flags are the contract, not the name=value pair.

Why

Session design. Frontend plates 231–233 are the browser side; this is the header.

Trap

Set-Cookie without Secure on an HTTPS site — still sent on a later HTTP navigation. Domain too wide. Two cookies jammed on one header line so one overwrites the other.
