BackendAdditive JSON · /v2 for breaks

Don't break /v1 in place.

Clients you do not ship keep calling GET /v1/users/1. Adding an optional field is additive — old parsers ignore unknown keys. Rename, remove, or change price: 30 (cents int) to "30.00" is breaking. Put the break on /v2 (or Accept: application/vnd.shop.v2+json, or a Stripe-style date) and freeze /v1.

Same URL

Additive

GET /v1/users/1 { "name": "Ada", "email": "a@x" }

New optional field. New endpoint. Old clients ignore unknown keys. Not a new version.

Old client is wrong

Breaking

name → fullName price: 30 → "30.00" remove email email now required

Rename, remove, change type, new required field, new meaning, new error shape.

Put the break here

/v2

GET /v1/users/1 frozen GET /v2/users/1 new shape Sunset: 2027-01-01

URI /v1, or Accept, or date. /v1 keeps working until sunset. One version until you must.

What

Version in the path, a header, or a date. Additive = optional fields, new routes, new codes the old client can ignore (plate 34). Breaking = the old binary is wrong without a change.

Why

Mobile apps, other teams, and scripts do not ship on your Friday. A public JSON field is a contract. Interviews: one additive example, one breaking, and what you do with /v1.

Trap

Breaking fields in place: price was cents-int, now a string. Deprecating with no sunset date. Versioning on day one — stay on one until a real break.
