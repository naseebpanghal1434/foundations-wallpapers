DSAO(n²) or O(n log n)

Patience, or DP of tails.

Longest increasing subsequence — not a subarray. O(n²): best ending at i. O(n log n): tails[len] is the smallest tail of that length (patience). tails’ length is the LIS length, not the LIS itself.

n = 10³ · ending at i

n² dp

[10,9,2,5,3,7,101,18] dp 1,1,1,2,2,3, 4, 4 LIS length 4 2,5,7,101 2,3,7,18 subsequence, not subarray — not contiguous

Always acceptable at n=10³. Ending-at-i is the state. Contiguous increasing is a scan, not this.

n = 10⁵ · patience piles

tails

2 → [2] 5 → [2,5] 3 → [2,3] 7 → [2,3,7] 18→ [2,3,7,18] length 4 [4,5,1] tails=[1,5] — not a subsequence

Strict: first tail ≥ x (bisect_left). Non-decreasing: bisect_right. Russian dolls: sort w asc, h desc, LIS on h.

What

Order, not adjacency. n=10³: n². n=10⁵: n log n. tails mixes piles — print the sequence with parent pointers, not by reading tails.

Why

LeetCode 300 / 354 (sort w asc, h desc, LIS on h). LCS is the two-string cousin (plate 190).

Trap

Contiguous (Kadane). Treating tails as the LIS. bisect_left vs right on ties. Sorting first — that destroys the sequence.
