SystemDelivery, not magic

At least once means twice.

Queues retry. A timeout after “charge $30” may have succeeded; the retry sends it again. At-least-once delivery is the default (SQS, Rabbit, Kafka). Exactly-once of your side effect is an idempotent consumer (plate 29), not a broker checkbox.

May lose

At-most-once

ack, then process or fire and forget

No duplicates. A crash between send and work drops the message. Metrics, logs, “best effort.”

May duplicate

At-least-once

process, then ack timeout → retry two charges

The honest default. Producer retries. Consumer dies after work, before ack. You will see the message twice.

Effectively once

Exactly-once

at-least-once + unique key / upsert same charge id

The broker can help inside itself. Your DB write, email, Stripe call still need a key you can safely do twice.

What

At-most-once = lose rather than dup. At-least-once = retry until ack. Exactly-once = at-least-once plus idempotent handling (unique constraint, upsert, outbox). Kafka transactions are consume-transform-produce inside Kafka, not “Stripe ran once.”

Why

Networks lie (plate 19). If billing is a queue (plate 06), duplicates are the design. Interviews want: name the three, pick at-least-once, put the uniqueness on the consumer.

Trap

“We use Kafka, so we have exactly-once everywhere.” EOS is not your HTTP handler, not your email, not a row without a unique key. Auto-commit-before-work is silent at-most-once. Deduping only in RAM dies on restart.
