Toolsulimit -c · gdb

A core dump is the address space at death.

SIGSEGV/SIGABRT with ulimit -c unlimited writes a file of the mm plus notes (registers, auxv). gdb ./app core then bt; systemd-coredump or apport may intercept. Size ≈ RSS — strip secrets; without debug symbols you get hex.

What

A snapshot for postmortem. Not a log. The file is the address space plus register notes at the killing signal.

Why

Native crashes. You cannot replay the race; you can read the heap and the stack that remained.

Trap

core_pattern piped to nowhere. Shipping without symbols and wondering why bt is empty. Core files in /tmp with secrets in RAM.
