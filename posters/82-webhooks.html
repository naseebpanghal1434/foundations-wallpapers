What

Inbound HTTP callback with a shared secret. Store event_id; a replay returns 200 and does not charge twice. You did not poll — they pushed.

Why

You cannot poll Stripe for every charge. They tell you, and they retry when you time out (plate 19). Interviews: signature, raw body, fast ack, idempotency key = event id.

Trap

No signature check — anyone POSTs a fake invoice.paid. Slow handler inside the request: they time out, retry, you double-send. 500 on a duplicate instead of 200.
