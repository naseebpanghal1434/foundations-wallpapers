SystemEncode · HLS · CDN

Video is chunks, not a file.

A 4K title is not GET /movie.mp4 from origin. Upload to blob (plate 207), transcode 360p–1080p, pack 2–6 s HLS/DASH segments (.ts + .m3u8). The player picks a bitrate; the CDN holds hot segments. Origin is the miss.

CDN holds hot segmentsPlayer ABR picks the ladder. Pre-warm titles. Premiere on origin with no CDN is a stampede (plate 94).

edge
What

Adaptive bitrate + 2–6 s segments + CDN. Metadata (duration, titles) in the DB. The player is the bitrate load balancer; origin is the miss.

Why

YouTube / Netflix design. They want: transcode async, segments, CDN — not a single MP4 progressive download.

Trap

One giant file. Transcoding on the upload request. No 360p ladder. Origin without a CDN on the first frame of a premiere.
