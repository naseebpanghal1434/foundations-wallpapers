Database2^32 · autovacuum_freeze

Transaction IDs wrap. Freeze is not optional.

Postgres tuple xids are 32-bit. Age past ~2e9 and unfrozen tuples look in the future — rows appear to vanish. VACUUM FREEZE writes frozen xids. autovacuum_freeze_max_age (default 200e6) must run. Watch txid_current() and age(datfrozenxid).

What

A circular 32-bit counter plus freeze. Operational, not academic. Frozen means “older than every snapshot, stop comparing xid.”

Why

The outage that looks like “the table is empty” or “cannot start transactions.” Logs screamed about wraparound for days first.

Trap

A long-open transaction blocking freeze. Ignoring wraparound warnings. A replica or forgotten database that never vacuums.
