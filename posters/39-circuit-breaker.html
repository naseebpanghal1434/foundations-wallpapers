Systemclosed · open · half-open

Stop calling a sick dependency.

A circuit breaker is a state machine in front of an RPC. Closed: calls flow; trip if e.g. 50% of the last 20 fail or p99 > 2 s. Open: fail immediately (503 / fallback) for 30 s — don’t occupy a thread. Half-open: 1–5 probes; success closes, failure reopens.

Healthy · traffic flows

Closed

Count failures in a sliding window. Trip: 50% of last 20, or 5 consecutive, or timeout budget. Timeouts still apply (e.g. 2 s).

Sick · fail fast

Open

Do not dial. Return fallback or 503 now. 200 threads × 30 s hang is the outage; 0 calls is the recovery window.

Probe after cooldown

Half-open

After 30 s, allow 1–5 calls. Success → closed. Failure → open again. Resilience4j / Hystrix picture.

What

State machine wrapping an RPC. Pair with bulkheads (separate pools) so payments dying does not exhaust the app’s connections (plate 03). Fallback: last-good cache, default, or error — pick one on purpose.

Why

Cascading failure: A waits on B, A’s threads fill, C waits on A, the site is gone. The breaker isolates. Interview staple next to retries and timeouts.

Trap

Opening on 1 error. No fallback (empty 500 vs cached last-good). One global breaker for all tenants. Retrying after the breaker already failed fast — undoes the protection.
