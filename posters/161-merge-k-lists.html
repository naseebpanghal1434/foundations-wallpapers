DSAk-way merge

A heap of k heads.

Merge k sorted lists: a min-heap of the current heads. Pop smallest, push its next. n nodes, heap size k → O(n log k). Sequential merge is O(kn).

n pops · heap size k

1→4→5 1→3→4 2→6 heap {1, 1, 2} pop 1, push 4 pop 1, push 3 … 1-1-2-3-4-4-5-6 O(n log k)

Entry is (val, list id, node) — nodes are not comparable. Dummy head for the result. Arrays: heap of (value, list-id, index).

What
