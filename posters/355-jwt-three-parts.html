JWT is header.payload.signature.

A JWT is three base64url segments: header.payload.signature. Header has alg and typ; payload claims (sub, exp, iat, iss, aud) are readable by anyone; the signature is HMAC or RSA over header.payload. Verify alg, iss, aud, exp — the payload is not encrypted (that’s JWE).

What

A signed set of claims. Stateless if you don’t revoke; revocation needs a denylist or short exp + refresh.

Why

OAuth access tokens, session substitutes. Why you still need TLS, and why alg=none was a CVE.

Trap

Trusting payload before sig. Accepting alg from the header blindly. Storing PII in JWT and logging it (plate 271). A 10-year exp.
