DSAFloyd step two

Meet, then walk from head.

Where the cycle begins: first find a meeting point (plate 144). Then put one pointer at head, keep the other at the meeting point, walk both by one. They meet at the entrance. Why: if head-to-start is a, start-to-meet is b, cycle is c, then a = kc − b — the remaining walk around equals a.

What

Two-phase Floyd. Phase 1 detects; phase 2 locates. You do not need to recite the full proof — state a equals the remaining distance around the cycle. O(n) time, O(1) space.

Why

LeetCode 142. Also: duplicate in a 1..n array as a linked list of indices. Interviewers want the second phase, not just “I would store nodes in a set.”

Trap

Resetting both pointers to head. Walking fast at speed 2 in phase 2. Treating the meeting node as the entrance. Mutating next to detect (ruins the list). Off-by-one proofs you don’t need — the walk is the proof.
