DatabaseDouble entry · balance is a sum

Money is a ledger, not a field.

UPDATE accounts SET balance = balance - 10 is a lost update (plate 73) and no audit. A ledger is append-only — WAL for money (plate 95): debit A, credit B, same COMMIT, integer cents. Balance is SUM(entries) or snapshot + tail. Refund = opposite rows, not DELETE.

The field is a cache of the sum. Subtract-then-add without a transaction (plate 7) loses money in the gap.

Append-only · cents

Ledger

tx 991 same COMMIT ada −1000 ¢ store +1000 ¢ UNIQUE (idempotency_key) snapshot nightly refund = opposite rows

Money is integer cents, not float. Currency is a column. Outbox if you also notify (plate 224). Isolation: serializable or a version on the snapshot (plates 40, 76).

What

Append-only, double-entry, idempotent inserts (plate 225). You can recompute. You cannot un-say a row — reverse with a new one. The COMMIT is the WAL.

Why

Payments / wallet. They want: never mutate money in place. Audit is the product.

Trap

FLOAT dollars. Deleting a charge. Balance field as the only truth. Two-step subtract-then-add without a transaction. Using wall-clock as the unique id.
