BackendCardinality · metrics vs logs

Labels explode the time series.

A Prometheus series is a metric name + label set. http_requests{code,route,method,region} is bounded. {user_id=…} is 1 series per user — 10M series is RAM death. High-cardinality belongs in logs or traces (plate 84). Histograms, not a mean you cannot undo.

Counters · histograms

Bounded

http_requests_total {code, route, method, region} histogram_request_seconds 0.05 0.1 0.25 1 5 exemplar → trace id scrape 15s, downsample

Histograms for latency. Exemplars link a spike to a trace. Cardinality is the product constraint.

vs

10M series · TSDB RAM

user_id

http_requests{user_id=…} http_requests{email=…} http_requests{request_id=…} 1 series per user 1 per request the bill is the alert

user_id is a log field. request_id is a trace. Neither is a Prometheus label. URL-with-id is the same bomb.

What

Metrics: cheap, aggregated, bounded label set. Logs: unbounded text. Traces: per-request path. Downsample old data; do not keep scrape resolution forever.

Why

Observability design. SDE-2s blow the metrics cluster in week one with a well-meant user_id label.

Trap

user_id / URL-with-id as a label. Averages without histograms. One log line per metric scrape. Infinite retention at scrape resolution.
