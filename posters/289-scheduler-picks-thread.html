OSCFS · rq · timeslice

The scheduler picks the next thread.

On each timer tick (~1–4 ms HZ) or a blocking syscall, the CPU’s runqueue picks a TASK_RUNNING thread. Linux CFS: vruntime, red-black tree, not a fixed slice. SCHED_FIFO/RR are realtime: they preempt CFS until they block. Idle is the swapper thread.

What

Runnable vs sleeping (TASK_INTERRUPTIBLE). Blocking I/O takes you off the rq.

Why

Latency vs throughput. A CPU hog starves others until the tick. RT for audio, not for HTTP.

Trap
