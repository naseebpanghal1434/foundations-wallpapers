BackendCSRF · SSRF · copilot

The confused deputy has your power, their intent.

A deputy (your browser, your server, your LLM) is allowed to do X; an attacker aims it at Y. CSRF (plate 30), SSRF (plate 358), and prompt injection (plate 46) are the same pattern: ambient authority plus untrusted input.

Browser · cookies

CSRF

cookie is ambient evil POST /transfer server thinks it's you

SameSite + CSRF token. Authority was the cookie, not the intent.

Server · URL

SSRF

GET(userUrl) 169.254.169.254 VPC trust is ambient

Allowlist hosts. The server’s net is the power; the URL is their intent.

LLM · tools

Prompt

mail in context model calls DELETE tool auth is ambient

Allowlists, human confirm. Don’t give the model a loaded gun.

What

Confused deputy problem (Norm Hardy). Fix authority — capability tokens, CSRF tokens, allowlists, human confirm — don’t just filter strings.

Why

Unifies a lot of “security bugs” in one sentence: ambient authority plus untrusted input.

Trap

Adding a WAF regex and calling it done. An LLM with a DELETE tool and untrusted mail in context.
