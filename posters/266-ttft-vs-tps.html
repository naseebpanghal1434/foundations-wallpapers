TTFT is wait. TPS is the stream.

TTFT is prefill: eat the prompt, build KV (plate 254). TPS is decode — one token at a time — so a huge prompt wrecks first-token wait even if the stream looks fast.

What

Two metrics, two stages. Optimize prompt size for TTFT; batching and smaller models for TPS. Stream; don’t buffer the essay.

Why

UX and capacity. “The model is slow” is which one — first token, or tokens per second?

Trap
