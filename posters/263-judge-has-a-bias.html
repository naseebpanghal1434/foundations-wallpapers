A judge model has a bias too.

A second model grading answers is noisy: it prefers whoever is listed first, and the longer write-up. Use it when code cannot score; swap A/B, average, and prefer a checklist (“contains X”) over 1–5 stars.

What

A noisy metric. Fine for ranking prompts; bad as the only ship gate. Keep a human spot-check on a slice.

Why

Evals at scale (plate 262). Interviews: name the bias — position, length — and how you cancel it.

Trap

Letting the judge see the gold and the model’s chain-of-thought and calling it blind. One call, one number, ship.
