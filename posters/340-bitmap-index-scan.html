DatabaseBitmapHeapScan

Bitmap AND of two indexes, then heap.

Two moderately selective predicates: Postgres builds a bitmap per index, AND/ORs them, then visits heap pages in physical order (almost sequential). Cheaper than two index scans that bounce randomly; cheaper than seq if the bitmaps stay sparse.

Bitmap Index Scan idx_astatus = 'open' → bits of matching TIDs. One index, one bitmap.

Bitmap Heap ScanWalk surviving heap pages in order, then Recheck Cond. Sequential-ish.

heap
What

Combine single-column indexes without (a,b). Still not magic if both predicates match 40% of the table.

Why

AND of two filters. A composite still wins for a,b together (plate 97) — one seek, no bitmap tax.

Trap

Creating 12 single-column indexes and expecting every AND to be free. Ignoring “lossy” in EXPLAIN: extra heap filter, worse than it looks.
