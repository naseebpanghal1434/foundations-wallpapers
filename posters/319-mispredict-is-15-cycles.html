Archbranch · pipeline squash

A mispredict is ~15 cycles.

The front end guesses the branch so fetch can continue. Correct: ~1 cycle. Wrong: squash ~10–20 cycles of work (~15 typical; 3 GHz → ~5 ns). Unpredictable branches in a hot loop (50/50 data) cost more than extra arithmetic. Sorting or bucketing to make branches boring helps.
