DSAk steps right · O(1) space

Reverse all. Reverse the parts.

Rotate right by k: last k items move to the front. k %= n. Extra array is O(n) space. In-place: reverse the whole array, reverse [0,k), reverse [k,n). Each item walks to its final index. Cycle-swap (jump i → i+k) is the other O(1) write — gcd cycles.

What

Right rotate: a[i] ← a[(i−k) mod n]. Three reverses prove it: reverse puts the tail block at the front (backwards), then each block is unreversed. Extra array b[(i+k)%n]=a[i] is correct and uses O(n) space.

Why

LeetCode 189. Tests modulo and in-place thinking. Rotate a matrix 90° is a different plate (197) — transpose then reverse rows, not this.

Trap

Forgetting k %= n (k=n should be identity; k>n otherwise index error). Reversing [0,k] inclusive — off-by-one. Using extra memory after O(1) space was required. Rotating left vs right.
