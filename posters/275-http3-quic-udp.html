HTTP/3 is QUIC on UDP.

HTTP/3’s transport is QUIC on UDP, with TLS 1.3 baked in (plate 279). Streams are independent: a lost packet stalls one stream, not the connection — unlike TCP plus HTTP/2.

What

HTTP semantics on a new transport. Connection migration (IP change) is a QUIC feature. Fall back to H2 is normal.

Why

Mobile networks, lossy links. “Why UDP if UDP is unreliable?” Reliability moved into QUIC.

Trap

Blocking UDP/443 and blaming the app. 0-RTT for POST /pay (replay). Treating H3 as faster CPU — it can cost more CPU.
