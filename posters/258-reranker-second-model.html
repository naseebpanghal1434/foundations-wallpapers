A reranker is a second, smaller model.

A bi-encoder embeds query and doc separately — fast, cacheable. A cross-encoder reads the pair and scores relevance; that second model is the reranker — it sorts, it does not retrieve.

Two vectors

Cosine

cosine(q, d) cheap, approximate cache each side

Bi-encoder. First-stage retrieval. Fast over millions.

vs

One pair

Cross-encoder

model([q; d]) → score 50 pairs, not 1e6

Distilled rerankers (miniLM) are usual. The LLM can rerank, at 10× cost.

What

Pairwise relevance on a shortlist. Cache nothing about the pair except the score for that query.

Why

The missing piece after “we did embeddings.” SDE-2 GenAI.

Trap

Cross-encoding the whole corpus. Using the generator as the only ranker with a 20k prompt. A news reranker applied to code.
