Archalignas · packed

Unaligned access splits the load.

A 4-byte load at address 2 crosses a 4-byte (and maybe 64 B line) boundary: two loads + merge, or a fault on strict ISAs (classic SPARC, some ARM). x86 allows it and charges you. Packed structs (__attribute__((packed))) create this on purpose.

packed {u8, u32}

Offset 1

u8 at 0 u32 at 1 ← split may cross 64 B line strict ISA: fault x86: two loads + merge

Casting a byte buffer to uint64_t* at offset 3 is this.

vs

Natural alignment

Pad to 4

u8 at 0 pad 3 bytes u32 at 4 alignof(double)=8 malloc ≥ 16 (64-bit glibc)

SIMD wants 16/32-byte align. Padding is the compiler buying cheap loads.

What

Natural alignment = size. Padding is the compiler buying cheap loads.

Why

ABI, serialization vs in-memory structs, mmap of on-disk packed formats.

Trap

Casting a byte buffer to uint64_t* at offset 3. packed plus taking addresses of members and passing them to APIs that assume align.
