DSAdiff[l]+=x · diff[r+1]−=x

A range add is two point writes.

To add x on [l,r] many times, do not loop the range. Write diff[l]+=x and diff[r+1]−=x. After all updates, a[i] = a[i-1] + diff[i] reconstructs the array in one pass. Each update O(1), build O(n).

Prefix oncea[i] = a[i-1] + diff[i] → 1,3,3,3,1. Corporate flight bookings, range addition, car pooling (capacity as a diff on trips).

integrate
What

Difference is the inverse of prefix (plate 126). Point writes on the derivative, then integrate. 2D: four corners of a rectangle (+x at top-left, −x just past right and just past bottom, +x at the far corner).

Why

Many range updates, one final array. Car pooling: +passengers at start, − at end, scan the prefix to see if cap holds. Calendar busy times. Do not walk i=l..r per update.

Trap

Looping the range anyway. r+1 off the array — allocate n+1. Mixing 0-based events with 1-based bookings. Using this when you also need random range queries in between — then Fenwick (plate 121).
