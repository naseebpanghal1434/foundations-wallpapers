DSAArea = min(h) × width

The short side moves.

Container with most water: two lines form a tank, area = min(h[lo], h[hi]) * (hi − lo). Start at both ends. The water is limited by the shorter line — moving the taller cannot help (width shrinks, min height cannot rise). Move the short side. O(n).

One tank · not unit wells

[1,8,6,2,5,4,8,3,7] lo=0 hi=8 min(1,7)*8 = 8 move lo min(8,7)*7 = 49 best min(8,3)*6 = 18 move hi … best 49

Any pair inside with the current short side is worse: smaller width, height ≤ that short. Greedy on the bottleneck.

Not trapping rain (plate 155). One rectangle between two lines, not a well on every index.

What

Two pointers on unsorted heights (the order is the geometry, not a sort — plate 54). Proof: the current short side cannot form a better tank with anyone inside the range. So it can be discarded.

Why

LeetCode 11. Two-pointer poster child. Say “move the short side” before you type the loop. Area is min-height times width, once, for the pair you chose.

Trap

Sorting the heights — destroys indices and width. Moving both pointers. Moving the tall side. Using this for rain water (unit bars, both maxes, plate 155).
