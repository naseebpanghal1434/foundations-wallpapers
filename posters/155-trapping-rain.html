DSATwo pointers or prefix max

Water is min of both maxes, minus height.

Each index holds min(leftMax, rightMax) − height, if positive. Two pointers: the shorter side is the bottleneck — settle it and walk inward. Not container-with-most-water (plate 156).

Two pointers

lo=0 hi=n-1 Lmax, Rmax if height[lo] < height[hi]: settle lo using Lmax # right wall ≥ height[hi] lo += 1 else: settle hi using Rmax

Not container-with-most-water (plate 156). One tank vs unit-width wells. O(n) time, O(1) extra.

What

Water cannot slope out over a taller min-wall. Two pointers settle the short side: its max-so-far is the ceiling.

Why

LeetCode 42. Name the formula before the pointers. One tank (plate 156) is a different problem.

Trap

Min of neighbors, not max-so-far. Moving the taller pointer. 2D trap (LC 407) is a min-heap BFS from the border.
