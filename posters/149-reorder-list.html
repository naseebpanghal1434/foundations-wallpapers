DSAL0 Ln L1 Ln-1

Split. Reverse the back. Weave.

Reorder list to L0→Ln→L1→Ln−1…. Three algorithms you already have: slow/fast finds the mid and cuts. Reverse the second half (plate 145). Merge by weaving (one from each). Palindrome list is split + reverse + compare instead of weave.

Reverse the back (plate 145)4-5 becomes 5-4. Save nxt first. Do not reverse including the mid on an odd list — the mid would point at itself.

3

Weave with a temp for next1-2-3 and 5-4 → 1-5-2-4-3. Palindrome (LC 234): compare first half vs reversed second, no weave. Array of nodes is O(n) space — they usually want O(1).

merge
What

Compose primitives. O(n) time, O(1) space. Find mid, cut, reverse, weave. Even/odd: for odd, the mid stays in the first half so the two lists differ by at most one.

Why

LeetCode 143 / 234. Interview: can you sequence three known tricks without mixing the pointers. If you can reverse and merge, this is those two plus a cut.

Trap

Not cutting the first half — a cycle. Reversing including the mid on odd lists so the mid points at itself. Weaving without a temp for next. Using an array of nodes after they asked O(1) space.
