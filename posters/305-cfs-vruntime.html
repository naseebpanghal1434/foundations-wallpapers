CFS tracks vruntime, not a fixed slice.

Completely Fair Scheduler: each task’s vruntime increases as it runs, slower if weight is higher (nice −20). Pick the leftmost (lowest vruntime) in the rb tree. sched_latency_ns (~6–24 ms) is the target period to cycle runnable tasks, not a hard slice.
