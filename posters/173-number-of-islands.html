Mutate in place or a visited matrix. Each cell visited once. Connected components on a grid graph (plate 118).

What

One flood per island. 4-dir is the default unless they say 8. The scan finds components; the flood paints them. Union-find is the same count of roots after uniting adjacent land.

Why

LeetCode 200. The grid DFS template. Everything that “paints a region” (area, surrounded, pacific-atlantic) is this plus a different start set. Interviews: increment once per flood, not once per 1.

Trap

8-direction when the spec is 4. Recursion on 1000×1000. Counting every 1, not every component. Visiting water. Off-by-one bounds. Forgetting to mark, so you infinite-loop the same land.
