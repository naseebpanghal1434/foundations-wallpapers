Backendkid · grace

Two keys live during rotation. One dies after.

Rotate a signing or encryption key: generate new, sign with new, verify with both for a grace window, then drop old. JWT kid header selects the key from JWKS. Dual-read, single-write; secrets in env/files need the same two-phase deploy.
