BackendSIGTERM · 30s · SIGKILL

Drain, then close the pool.

kubelet sends SIGTERM, waits terminationGracePeriodSeconds (default 30 s), then SIGKILL. Catch SIGTERM: fail /healthz, server.close() (no new sockets), finish in-flight, pool.end(), exit(0). SIGKILL does not run finally or COMMIT. A kill mid-write is a 502 and a transaction that may or may not have committed.

What

Stop the HTTP server first so the load balancer stops sending. Wait in-flight with a timeout shorter than the grace period. Then close pools. Order matters.

Why

Rolling deploys and scale-in kill processes on purpose. Without drain, every ship 502s checkouts in flight and leaves rows half-written (plate 07).

Trap

Ignoring SIGTERM until SIGKILL tears the TCP connection. Closing the pool before requests finish. Hanging forever on a stuck call — the grace period still ends in SIGKILL.
