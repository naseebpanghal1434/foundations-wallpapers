BackendRED · OTel · traceparent

Logs tell. Metrics count. Traces follow.

Three telemetry signals. Logs are events: structured JSON with a request_id. Metrics are time series — Prometheus counters, gauges, histograms (http_requests_total, p99). Traces are a span tree; propagate W3C traceparent on every RPC. RED: rate, errors, duration. Same id in all three is how you jump from an alert to a story.

Event

Logs

order.paid id=88 request_id=7f3a. Why did this one fail? High volume. Not a paragraph.

Time series

Metrics

http_requests_total{code="500"} 42. p99 180 ms → 1400 ms. Alerts live here. Cheap to scrape.

Span tree

Traces

checkout 220 ms → payments 180 ms → db.query 160 ms. OpenTelemetry. Which hop was slow?

What

Logs: append-only events. Metrics: RED. Traces: parent/child spans. request_id belongs in logs and traces, not as a Prometheus label.

Why

“It’s slow” is a trace. “How often?” is a metric. “What happened to order 88?” is a log. On-call with only one of them is guessing.

Trap

PII in logs: email, tokens, Authorization, whole req.body. High-cardinality labels: user_id / email on a metric → unbounded series, melts the TSDB.
