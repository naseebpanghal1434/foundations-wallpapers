DSALongest repeating · k flips

You may flip k. That's still a window.

Longest repeating character replacement: you may change up to k letters. In a window, keep the count of the most frequent char (maxf). The rest must be flipped. Valid iff window_len − maxf ≤ k; else shrink left. The window never needs to shrink more than one — we only want the longest.

1

s=AABABBA k=1. Walk the windowsAABA — maxf=3, len=4, 4−3=1 ≤k. AABAB — 5−3=2 >k, shrink. Best 4 (AABA or ABBA).

example
2

Valid: len − maxf ≤ kMode of the window is the letter you keep. You do not pick the letter first — the counts do. k=0 is just the longest run.

invariant
3

Stale maxf on shrink is fineDo not recompute it down. It may sit high; we only care about max length, and a high maxf still caps the answer at maxf+k. Cousins: fruit into baskets, at most k distinct.

maxf
What

A variable window (plate 55) whose cost is “how many chars are not the mode.” One pass, O(n) time, 26 counts if alphabet is letters. Same bones as max consecutive 1s with k flips (LC 1004) — binary, still len − maxf ≤ k.

Why

LeetCode 424. Teaches “window cost = len − best.” Interviews want the formula before the code. If they ask which letter you flip toward: the mode of the current window.

Trap

Restarting a DP / inner scan — O(n²). Forgetting k=0. Thinking you must choose the letter globally first. Updating maxf down on shrink is optional; if you skip it, still correct for the longest. Nested max over the 26 counts every step is fine; the stale shortcut is faster to write.
