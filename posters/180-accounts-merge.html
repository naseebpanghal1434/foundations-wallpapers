DSADSU on emails

Same email? Same person. Union.

Accounts merge: if two accounts share an email, they are the same person. Union-Find on emails (or on account ids). Map email→first account id, union when the email is already seen. Then gather emails by find(root), sort, prefix the name. Graph: emails as nodes, BFS components — same thing.

Union on emails, never namesJohn: a@, b@ and John: b@, c@ → one pile {a,b,c}. Two Johns with no shared email stay two people.

Emails as graph nodes, edge if they co-occur, BFS each component: same answer, more memory. Name is a label. Equality is the email.

What

Connected components with a dynamic “same as” relation. DSU on the emails (or account ids keyed by email). O(n α(n) + sort). Undirected equality — not a directed “points to.”

Why

LeetCode 721. The DSU application problem. Also: stones removed, similar string groups. If they say “merge if they share a key,” you said union-find before you said hashmap-of-lists.

Trap

Union names not emails (two Johns). Forgetting to sort. Directed union. Building n² pairwise email compares instead of a map email→id.
