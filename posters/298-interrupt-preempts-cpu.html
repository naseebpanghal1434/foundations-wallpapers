OSIRQ · bottom half

An interrupt preempts the CPU, not your if.

A device raises an IRQ line. The CPU jumps to the vector; the kernel runs the ISR (top half, short), then softirq/tasklet/workqueue (bottom half). Your userspace if() is not atomic against this — only against other threads if you lock. cli/sti is kernel-only.
