DSAMax-low · min-high

Two heaps. Balanced sizes.

Running median: a max-heap of the lower half, a min-heap of the upper half. Invariant: every low ≤ every high, and the sizes differ by at most 1. Insert: push to low, move low.top to high if needed, then rebalance sizes. Median is low.top (odd) or the average of both tops (even).

Don’t sort the stream each time. Sliding-window median needs lazy deletion or a balanced BST (plate 125) — two heaps alone don’t evict the leaving index.

What

O(log n) insert, O(1) median. Two priority queues, not a sorted list. The extra element on odd counts sits on the max-heap so the middle is always LOW.top. Rebalance after every insert; never let LOW.top sneak past HIGH.top.

Why

LeetCode 295. Hard design. They are testing the invariant, not whether you can sort on findMedian. Follow-up is a window, not a stream — different structure.

Trap

Both min-heaps. Allowing LOW.top > HIGH.top. Even/odd size rule flipped so you average the wrong pair. Integer vs float average. Inserting always into the same heap and hoping.
