FrontenduseEffect cleanup · SPA

A leak is a listener you never remove.

In an SPA the page never unloads. A window listener, a setInterval, a subscription — they live until you cut them. useEffect’s return is the cut.

1

Subscribe on setupconst id = setInterval(…). window.addEventListener('resize', onR). Empty deps: once per mount.

setup
2

Return the cutclearInterval(id). removeEventListener('resize', onR) — same function reference, not a new arrow.

cleanup
3

Leave without it, keep the treeNavigate away and you duplicate listeners or retain the old fiber. AbortController for fetch. Closures hold props (plate 23).

SPA
What

Cleanup mirrors setup. Detached DOM + a listener is a leak. Closures hold the fiber’s props.

Why

SPAs don’t get the free GC of a full navigation (plate 70). Slow after 20 route changes.

Trap

addEventListener in render. remove with a different arrow. setInterval without clear. Subscribing to a store in an effect with no unsubscribe.
