Preorder names the root. Inorder splits.

Construct a tree from preorder and inorder. Preorder[0] is the root. Find it in inorder: left of it is the left subtree, right is the right. Recurse with matching slices of preorder. A hashmap value→inorder index makes the find O(1). O(n). Postorder+inorder: the root is last in postorder.

What

Preorder gives roots in time. Inorder gives the split. Left size = how many preorder cells after the root belong to the left child. Recurse both sides with those ranges.

Why

LeetCode 105 / 106. Tests recursion indices harder than invert-tree. Interviews want the map and the left-size arithmetic, not n slices that copy arrays.

Trap

Slicing preorder the wrong length (left size = rootIndex − inLo, not “half”). Scanning inorder every call → O(n²). Using postorder as if it were preorder. Building from inorder alone.
