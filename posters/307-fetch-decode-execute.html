Archpipeline · PC

Fetch, decode, execute. Then write back.

The CPU fetches the instruction at PC, decodes opcode+regs, executes in the ALU/LSU, writes back registers, then PC+=len (or a branch). Pipelines overlap those stages. A 3 GHz core is 0.33 ns/cycle; when the pipe is full, 1–4 instructions retire per cycle.
