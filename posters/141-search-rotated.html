pivot
What

Still a binary search with an extra sorted-half test. Finding the rotation index then ordinary search is two log n passes — also correct. Unique elements: O(log n). Duplicates: you may have to shrink by one.

Why

LeetCode 33 / 81 / 153. Interview favorite because the invariant is easy to get almost right. Draw the two halves and point at the sorted one before you move lo/hi.

Trap

Assuming both halves are sorted. Using a[0] as the split when there are duplicates. Off-by-one on inclusive ranges. Not handling already-sorted (rotation 0). On a[lo]==a[mid]==a[hi], guessing a half instead of shrinking by 1.
