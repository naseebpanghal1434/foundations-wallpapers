What

Multimodal = extra tokens with a spatial layout. They eat the same budget as the prompt and the answer.

Why

Copilots that “see” the IDE. Cost surprises. Tile count is the knob, not megapixels of pride.

Trap

Sending a 12MP photo for a 20-word answer. Assuming the model reads 6px font in a full-page shot. Tiles can drop the corner you cared about.
