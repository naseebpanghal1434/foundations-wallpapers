LangC/C++ · as-if

UB is a license, not a crash.

Undefined behavior in C/C++: the standard imposes no requirements. The compiler may delete the branch, “format your disk” in theory, or appear to work until -O2. Signed overflow, use-after-free, data races, out-of-bounds — not a guaranteed SIGSEGV.

C/C++ · as-if · -O2

No rules

int x = *p; if (p) use(p); ↑ gone: *p already proved p non-null i++ past INT_MAX UAF · race · OOB

Nasal demons is the joke. Deleted checks are the reality. -fsanitize=undefined,address.

vs

Defined · panic or wrap

A rule

Rust panic / wrap Java throw / wrap Go panic / wrap C unsigned wrap is defined null deref → fault in these runtimes

They still have bugs. They do not give the optimizer a license to delete your if.

What

A hole in the spec the optimizer exploits under the as-if rule. If signed overflow “cannot happen,” a loop to INT_MAX may never terminate.

Why

Security: RCE from a “benign” overflow. Why sanitizers exist, and why -O0 tests are not the shipping program.

Trap

Testing -O0 and shipping -O3. “It works on my compiler.” Using signed i++ to walk to INT_MAX. A data race on a non-atomic is UB, not a stale read (plate 330).
