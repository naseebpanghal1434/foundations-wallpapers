TLS 1.2 is about two RTTs of crypto after TCP. TLS 1.3 is a 1-RTT handshake — keys from the first flight — and 0-RTT on a session ticket can send data immediately, replayable, so not for POST /charge.

TLS 1.3 · ALPN h2/h3

1-RTT

TCP handshake 1.3 keys in first flight GET on the next RTT revisit + ticket: 0-RTT GET replayable

Same PKI. 0-RTT is a cache of secrets with replay. ALPN picks h2 or h3.

What

Fewer RTTs, same certificates. 0-RTT early data is not a new trust model — verify the cert anyway.

Why

Tail latency on mobile RTTs of 100ms+. Why CDNs terminate TLS near the user.

Trap

0-RTT for a non-idempotent POST (plate 29). Expired clocks on IoT. Assuming 1.3 means you can skip cert verification.
