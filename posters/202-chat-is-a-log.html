What

Write is append. Read is a range by snowflake id. Pagination is the last id. The socket is a replica of the tail; crash recovery is the log.

Why

WhatsApp / Messenger: conversation as a stream, total order, unread, and edit as a new event — not a mutation of the past.

Trap

One global messages table ordered by server now() — clocks skew, two rows swap. Polling every 1 s as the product. Storing the whole history in the socket process.
