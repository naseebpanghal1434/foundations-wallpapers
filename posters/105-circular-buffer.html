DSARing · bounded queue

Head and tail walk a circle.

A ring is a fixed array plus two indices. Push writes at tail, then tail = (tail+1) % cap. Pop reads at head, then head = (head+1) % cap. Full and empty must be distinguishable — store a count, or keep one slot empty.

cap=4 · count tells full

Ring

[ 7 | 2 | _ | _ ] h t count=2 push 9 → tail=3 [ 7 | 2 | 9 | _ ] pop → 7, head=1

No alloc on push. Overflow is a policy: reject, or overwrite the oldest.

wrap

Indices modulo cap

Wrap

[ 9 | 4 | _ | 8 ] 0 1 2 3 head=3 tail=1 logical order: 8, 9, 4

From head: a[3], a[0], a[1] → 8, 9, 4. The array order is not the queue order.

What

Bounded queue in O(1) time and O(1) extra memory after the array exists. Producer/consumer, sliding window of last k, audio buffers. Modulo wraps the indices.

Why

Interviews: implement a circular queue (LeetCode 622). Name head, tail, count (or the empty-slot rule). Bounded work queues in services are this.

Trap

head==tail meaning both empty and full — you cannot tell. Keep count, or a boolean, or waste one slot (full when (tail+1)%cap==head). Also: % on negative indices in some languages.
