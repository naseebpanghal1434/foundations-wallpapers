Arch64 B · fill

A cache line is 64 bytes.

Caches move 64-byte lines (x86; some ARM 64/128). A load of 4 bytes fills 64. Sequential int32 scan: 16 values per miss. Stride 64 bytes: a miss every element. Align hot structs to 64 if you do not want them split across two lines.

Hot a and b split across two lines → two misses. Same 64 B → one. Write-back: dirty line evicted later. clflush / prefetchnta.

What

The transfer unit is the 64 B line, not the load size. Write-back: the dirty line is written later.

Why

Array-of-structs vs struct-of-arrays. Packet headers. Page is 4 KiB; the line is 64 B.

Trap

Linked list of 16-byte nodes: a miss per node (~100 ns RAM). Splitting a hot counter across a line with unrelated data on another core.
