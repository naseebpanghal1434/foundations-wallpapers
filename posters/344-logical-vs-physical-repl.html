DatabaseWAL shipping · pgoutput

Physical ships pages. Logical ships rows.

Physical/streaming: the replica is the same bytes (block-for-block), same major version, read-only hot standby. Logical: decode WAL to row events, apply to another schema or version, selective tables. Logical is CDC, upgrades, heterogeneous. Physical is HA.

Same cluster, extra copy

Physical

primary WAL → streaming replica replay 8 KiB pages same major version hot standby reads failover target

Byte clone. You cannot skip a table. Lag is replay lag (plate 99).

vs

Row events, other shape

Logical

PUBLICATION → slot (pgoutput) → subscriber INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE CDC, major upgrades Debezium (plate 224)

Slots retain WAL until consumed. A stuck slot fills the disk.

What

Same-cluster clone vs an event stream of rows. Both can lag. Logical needs a replica identity (usually the PK) to emit UPDATEs.

Why

HA vs integration. Physical for “the other box has the same bytes.” Logical when the subscriber is not a twin.

Trap

Logical on a chatty table without replica identity. A replication slot left behind after dropping a consumer — WAL grows until the disk doesn’t.
