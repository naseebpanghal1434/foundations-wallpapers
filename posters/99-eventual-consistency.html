SystemReplica lag

The replica is allowed to lag.

Eventual consistency: if writes stop, replicas converge. Until they do, a read can be stale. Async Postgres replicas, Dynamo, DNS, caches. The product-shaped name is read-your-writes: after I save, my next GET should see it. A replica does not promise that.

Quorum (W+R > N) is a stronger sibling. Sync replica is CAP-CP (plate 11): you wait, or you error.

What

Eventual = copies catch up; no bound unless you add one. Read-your-writes / session / monotonic reads are extra guarantees you design (route, wait, or version). Replica lag is the concrete number (seconds under load, not “eventually a few ms”).

Why

Replicas scale reads (plate 37). They also serve yesterday. Interviews: don’t say “eventual means incorrect.” Say who can see stale, for how long, and where you refuse to (checkout, the profile you just saved).

Trap

Read-after-write on a replica (LB round-robin after POST). Treating eventual as ACID-C (plate 41). Assuming the lag is always tiny. Caching on top of a replica and stacking two windows of stale.
