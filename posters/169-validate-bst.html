DSAlow < node < high

Inorder must increase. Or bounds.

A BST is the whole left subtree < node < the whole right — not just the kids. Carry bounds, or walk inorder and insist values strictly increase (plate 57).

The classic counterexample

Bounds

5 / \ 1 6 / \ 4 7 kids of 5 look fine kids of 6 look fine 4 is in the right of 5 4 < 5 — invalid bounds at 6: (5, ∞) 4 fails (5, 6)

±∞ sentinels (Long in Java, None in Python). Not INT_MIN if the tree can hold INT_MIN.

or

BST inorder is sorted

Inorder

walk left, node, right prev = −∞ each val > prev else false 1, 5, 4, 6, 7 5 then 4 fail

Keep a prev pointer, not a compare-to-parent. Duplicates: LC 98 is strict <; some BSTs allow ≤ on one side — read it.

What

O(n). Bounds shrink as you descend. Inorder is the same fact: a BST’s inorder is sorted.

Why

LeetCode 98. If you cannot name 5 / 1 / 6 / 4 / 7, you have not validated a BST.

Trap

Checking only immediate children. INT_MIN colliding with a node. Inorder without a prev pointer — same kids-only bug.
