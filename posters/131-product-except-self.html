DSANo division · two passes

Prefix times suffix. Skip i.

Product of the array except a[i], without division (zeroes would die, and the spec forbids it). left[i] = product of a[0..i). right[i] = product of a[i+1..]. answer[i] = left[i]*right[i]. Then drop the extra arrays: one output array as left, multiply a running suffix from the right.

Running suffix, O(1) extraAfter the left pass ans=[1,1,2,6], suffix=1. i=3: *=1 suffix=4; i=2 *=4 suffix=12; i=1 *=12 suffix=24; i=0 *=24.

in-place
What

Prefix/suffix products. No division. left[i] does not include a[i]. The output array may count as free; a running suffix is the O(1)-extra write. Zeros fall out of the products — do not special-case unless you want to.

Why

LeetCode 238. A poster child for “don’t divide, precompute both sides.” Same shape as trapping rain two-pass max (plate 155): a left scan, a right scan, combine at i.

Trap

Division. Multiplying the whole array then dividing by a[i] (fails on 0, fails the spec). left[i] including a[i]. Using O(n) extra after they asked O(1) extra — mention the running suffix.
