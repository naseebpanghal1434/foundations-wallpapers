ArchSSE · AVX2 · NEON

SIMD is one instruction, N lanes.

Single Instruction Multiple Data: AVX2 ymm = 8× float32 or 32× i8 per instruction. Compilers auto-vectorize simple loops; intrinsics when they don’t. Data must be packed and aligned-ish (16/32 B; plate 320). Branchy per-element code will not vectorize.

SSE / NEON

16 B

4× f32 or 16× i8.

AVX2

32 B

8× f32. Common on 2020s x86.

AVX-512

64 B

One cache line. Can downclock the core.

8-wide f32

8 / cyc

Add per port when packed. Tail is scalar.

libc

SIMD

strlen / memcpy are already wide.

Stride becomes 8 or 16. Remainder is a scalar tail. simdjson, codecs, ML inner products live here.

What

Wide registers + wide ops. The loop stride becomes 8 or 16. Remainder scalar tail.

Why

Codecs, images, parsers (simdjson), ML inner products.

Trap

AVX-512 downclocking a whole core. Mixing ABI with/without AVX (state leak). Assuming auto-vec of pointer-chasing loops.
