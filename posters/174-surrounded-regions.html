What

Multi-source flood from the boundary. Mark the Os that can reach an edge, then flip whatever O remains. O(rows×cols) — each cell once. 4-dir, same grid graph as islands (plate 173). Don’t DFS each interior cell independently.

Why

LeetCode 130. Pattern: “survives if connected to X” → search from X. Walls and gates, rotting oranges: the same multi-source BFS, just a different border.

Trap

Flipping while you DFS from an interior O (you don’t yet know). 4 vs 8 dir. Forgetting all four borders. Leaving S on the board after the capture pass.
