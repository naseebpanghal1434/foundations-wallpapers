OSmm_struct · pid · fds

A process is an address space.

A process is pid + mm (the virtual map) + fd table + one or more threads. fork() duplicates the map (CoW, plate 292). execve() replaces the map and starts at _start. Threads (clone CLONE_VM) share mm and fds; each has its own stack and tid.
