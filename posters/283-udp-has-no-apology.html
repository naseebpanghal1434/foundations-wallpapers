NetworkDatagram · loss

UDP has no apology.

TCP is a stream with retries, order, and congestion control. UDP is a datagram: send, maybe arrive, maybe twice, maybe never, maybe out of order.

Loss waits

TCP

seq 1 2 3 4 4 lost hold 5, 6, 7 retransmit 4 then the rest HOL (plate 285)

Ordered, reliable, congestion-controlled. A late packet still blocks the line.

vs

Packet 4 drops

UDP

pkt 1 2 3 4 5 4 gone 5 still plays maybe 3 after 5 maybe 2 twice no retry built in

ICMP unreachables and PMTU still exist. “UDP is faster” is “UDP does less.”

What

No connection, no built-in retry. You own duplication and ordering. Firewalls treat it worse (plate 275).

Why

Use UDP when a late packet is worthless. That is why games and HTTP/3 use it — and why you do not put a bank transfer on raw UDP.

Trap

Reimplementing TCP badly on UDP. Assuming order. Sending 64KB datagrams (fragmentation).
