DSABFS · zigzag

A queue of the current level.

Level order: BFS. Queue starts with root. For each level, n = queue.length — pop exactly n nodes, push their kids, emit that n as a list. Zigzag: reverse every other collected level (or a deque, push front/back). Vertical order and right-side view are BFS with extra coordinates, or “last node in the level.”

DFS with a depth index into buckets also works; BFS is the natural write. O(n). Store (node, depth) if you skip the inner length loop.

What

The queue plate applied to trees (plate 59, plate 107). One number — the length at the start of the level — is the whole trick. Do not walk with a depth counter unless each entry carries its depth.

Why

LeetCode 102 / 103 / 199 / 107. First tree BFS they ask. Bottom-up is reverse the levels. Average of levels is the same loop with a sum. Interviews: say “snapshot the size” before you pop.

Trap

Not capturing length, so levels mix — 9 and 15 in the same list. Recursing “level order” as DFS without buckets. Zigzag reversing the queue itself (destroys BFS order of children).
